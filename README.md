Public Health Researcher × AI/ML Engineer. I build computational models and open-source research systems for infectious-disease intelligence, scientific agents, and verifiable evidence workflows.

**Current status**
- Incoming Ph.D. student in Epidemiology and Health Statistics at Xiamen University, starting September 2026.
- M.Sc. in Intelligent Technology, Macau University of Science and Technology, 2024–2026.

## Research focus

- **Infectious-disease forecasting and early warning** — multimodal time-series modeling, surveillance × web-search × meteorological fusion (e.g. [MAESTRO](https://github.com/Hylouis233/MAESTRO)).
- **Influenza ecology and spatiotemporal dynamics** — co-circulation, seasonality, environmental drivers (e.g. [MultiCoPat](https://github.com/Hylouis233/MultiCoPat)).
- **Mechanistic outbreak modelling** — ODE and Petri Net dual-model pipelines for small-sample outbreaks (e.g. [ODE-Petri-Chikungunya](https://github.com/Hylouis233/ODE-Petri-Chikungunya)).
- **Phylodynamics and viral evolution** — emerging doctoral direction.

## Selected first-author publications

- **Spatiotemporal instability of influenza seasonality during viral co-circulation.** *npj Systems Biology and Applications* (2026). DOI [10.1038/s41540-026-00729-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41540-026-00729-9).
- **Dual-model framework for chikungunya transmission modelling: ODE and Petri Net analysis of the 2025 Foshan outbreak.** *BMC Public Health* 26 (2026). DOI [10.1186/s12889-026-28110-9](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-026-28110-9).
- **MAESTRO (Multi-modal Adaptive Estimation for Temporal Respiratory Disease Outbreak).** arXiv preprint [arXiv:2509.08578](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08578) (2025). Evaluated on over 11 years of Hong Kong influenza data; reports R² = 0.956.

## Methods and tools

- **Modelling:** ODE, Petri nets, Monte Carlo simulation, spatiotemporal analysis.
- **Machine learning:** PyTorch, multimodal learning, time-series forecasting, LLM agents.
- **Research infrastructure:** retrieval, citation verification, evaluation pipelines.
- **Engineering:** Python, R, APIs, databases, containerized deployment.

I emphasise **locked evaluation, strong baselines, transparent negative results, and reproducible evidence chains**.
